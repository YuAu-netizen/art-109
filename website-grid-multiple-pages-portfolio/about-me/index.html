ᯓ★About Me

Andy Au is a multidisciplinary artist at San Jose State whose work bridges the gap between traditional tactile craftsmanship and modern digital media. He has utilized art as a vital bridge for communication, using his unique perspective to expand his boundaries and connect with the world around him.

In Andy’s early works, the animation video of motion-art about positive activities help people overcome difficulties in the 2020 pandemic. His personal illustrations show visual interpretations or give life to a character throughout its drawings and sketches, conveying a message or encouragement of positivity. He even blends his digital mediums into the mixed-media of pictorials, photography from real places and nature, spanning from emotional connection, intent, perspective, storytelling, and symbolism.

Today, Andy is an active member of the arts community, dedicated to using his wide-ranging skills to paint a picture and tell stories that resonate with people and to continue refining his reputation as a professional artist.
